HOLLOWBOUND · 100 MULTI-MATERIAL TILESETS

Each 1536 × 1024 RGBA PNG holds six 512 × 512 material blocks.
Read them left to right on the first row, then left to right on the second.
Each block uses the same 4 × 4 grid of 128 × 128 cells. The index maps block
positions to the texture briefs. Row and column numbers are one-based.

Block row 1   Foreground fill | Background fill | Floor top middle | Ceiling middle
Block row 2   Platform left   | Platform middle | Platform right   | Floor top left
Block row 3   Slope rises     | Slope falls      | Stairs rise      | Stairs fall
Block row 4   Ceiling left    | Ceiling right    | Floor top right  | Standalone platform

Both fills repeat horizontally and vertically. Middle platform, floor-top and
ceiling sections repeat horizontally and join the matching end caps. Shape
tiles use hard alpha and the PNGs should be sampled with nearest filtering.

Local y of straight walking surfaces is 32. Rising slopes run from y=96 at
the left to y=32 at the right. Rising stair treads sit at y=92, 76, 60 and 44.
Descending pieces mirror these elevations. Place matchingly offset landings
or adjoining elements at the intended step or slope endpoint elevation.
